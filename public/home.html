Closing this page stops nothing. It is a signpost, not the bridge. Captions, the queue and every overlay keep running for as long as the terminal that started it is open — the page can be closed, the machine it was opened on can be shut down, and the service carries on.

To actually stop, press Ctrl‑C in that terminal.
